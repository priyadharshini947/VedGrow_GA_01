
Prompt #4 — Email Generation

### Domain

Communication / Writing

### Prompt Technique

Role prompting

### Use Case

Generating a professional email to request leave from a college faculty member.

---

## 1. Bad Prompt

> Write an email for leave.

### Why is this a bad prompt?

The prompt does not specify who the email is for, the reason for leave, the dates, or the expected tone. The AI has to guess the missing details.

---

## 2. Bad Prompt Output

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to request leave from college due to personal reasons. Kindly grant me leave for a few days.

Thank you.

Yours sincerely,
Student

---

## 3. Optimized Prompt

> Act as a professional communication assistant.
>
> Write a polite and professional leave request email to a college faculty member.
>
> Include the following details:
>
> * Reason: Personal work
> * Leave date: 12 August 2026
> * Return date: 13 August 2026
> * Mention that I will complete any missed work.
> * Keep the email short and respectful.
> * Include a suitable subject line.
>
> The email should be suitable for a college student.

---

## 4. Optimized Prompt Output

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to request leave on 12 and 13 August 2026 due to personal work. I kindly request you to grant me leave for these two days.

I will make sure to complete any academic work that I miss during my absence.

Thank you for your consideration.

Yours sincerely,
[Your Name]

---

## 5. Why the Optimized Prompt Is Better

The optimized prompt improves the original prompt by:

1. **Defining a role** — The AI is instructed to act as a professional communication assistant.
2. **Clearly defining the task** — The purpose of the email is specified.
3. **Providing important details** — The reason and leave dates are given.
4. **Specifying the recipient** — The email is intended for a college faculty member.
5. **Defining the tone** — The email should be polite and professional.
6. **Adding an important requirement** — The student should mention completing missed work.
7. **Specifying the length** — The email should be short and clear.
8. **Defining the format** — A suitable subject line is required.

---

## 6. Prompt Engineering Principles Demonstrated

* Role prompting
* Task specification
* Context
* Constraints
* Tone specification
* Output requirements
* Audience specification
